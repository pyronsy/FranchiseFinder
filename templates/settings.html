Powers title discovery (TMDB) and list syncing (MDBList). The app can't scan any franchise until both are set.

LLM Provider (optional)

Powers the optional gap-finding pass that runs alongside the TMDB filter each scan. Leave the provider set to Disabled to skip this step entirely — the app works fine without it.

Need a key? Google Gemini and Groq both issue free API keys instantly with no credit card — just an email or Google/GitHub account.
